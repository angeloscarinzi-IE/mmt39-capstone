## The rule that decides the grade

The guidelines say it in capitals: **it is a must to establish the causes for the
results.**

> Scores badly: "Market share fell to 12%."
> Scores well: "Share fell 4 points because our GRPs dropped 30% while Company B raised
> theirs 50% — survey 11 confirms it."

Same fact. Different mark.

## Objectives must be measurable

- Not: "grow market share."
- Yes: "raise H share in market A from 56.6% to 62% by moving price to 8.00 and holding
  media at 8 insertions, adding €19.6M of gross margin."

## Standing answers to the questions the CEO will ask

| Question                                     | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Why three shifts?                            | Labour is charged per line-shift and sits inside standard cost, so cost per unit is identical at 1, 2 or 3 shifts. The only cost is 2 points of depreciation; matching the output at two shifts would need 33 lines, which breaches the 32-line cap |
| Why activate only 2 of 22 moisturiser lines? | 17,628,699 units are already in the warehouse against demand of 15,047,394. Running the other 20 would have spent €27.3M of crew cost and €36.9M of raw material to make stock we cannot sell — **€64.2M of cash**                                  |
| Why buy SMED?                                | It lifts utilisation 81.25% → 92.9% on lines we already own. Sunscreen goes 5,850,000 → 6,688,800 units and its standard cost falls €5.5604 → €5.1358. +€9.16M of contribution on €6.0M of capex — **payback 0.65 years**                           |
| Why prepay the loan?                         | A 1% penalty of €195,000 cancels €1.95M + €1.30M + €0.65M of 10% coupons across Years 2–4. Year 1 paid €3,060,000 of financial expense on money that closed the year unused                                                                         |
| Why drop the credit line?                    | 2% of the facility — 1% upfront and 1% service — is charged whether or not it is drawn. We paid €200,000 in Year 1 and drew nothing, against a €32M cash cushion                                                                                    |
| Why drop the training department?            | It cuts office turnover from €24,000 to €9,000 a leaver. Four leavers, so €60,000 of saving, for €722,000 of cost. It does nothing for sales-rep turnover, which is 4.5× larger                                                                     |
| Why own the sales network?                   | Wholesalers take 8.46% of retail price. On Year-1 sales that is €16.0M against €3.9M for our own managers and reps, and the gap widens with every price rise                                                                                        |
| Why no factoring?                            | §41 adds receivables back to available cash on 2 January, so declining it costs only intra-year timing — and we closed Year 1 with no overdraft and an untouched credit line                                                                        |
| Why no Poka Yoke?                            | €720,000 per line applied to every line ever installed — payback beyond the length of the game                                                                                                                                                      |
| Why is our debt ratio 3.3× the industry?     | It was, on a €26M loan we did not need. It is **zero** after the Year-2 prepayment                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Why is inventory so high?                    | Unsold units never deteriorate and are sold first next year (§3). €40,990,251 of it is production cost already paid; Year 2 converts 61% of it back into cash                                                                                       |
| Why did moisturiser lose market A?           | We were the **cheapest** and finished third. Company 2 sold 71% more at €3.00–4.00 above us with our campaign and our positioning. Price is not the driver; we have bought surveys 11, 13, 14 and 15 to identify what is                            |
