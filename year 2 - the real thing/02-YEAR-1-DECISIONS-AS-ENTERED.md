**One cause, one lesson.** The forecast assumed the moisturiser would clear at 100%. It cleared
at 45.2%. The error was not in the plant, the costs or the cash — capacity was forecast to the
unit, every hand correction to the model proved right, and the calibration now reproduces the
whole report exactly. It was entirely in the demand assumption for a product priced at €6.50 in
a market where the winner charged €9.50–10.50.

The €40,990,251 of moisturiser sitting in the warehouse is not a loss — only units sold are
expensed, so it is cash converted into stock, and it is what Year 2 sells. But it is why cash
closed at €46,902,208 rather than the forecast €111,774,267.
