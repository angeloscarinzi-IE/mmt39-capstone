## SUBJECT DESCRIPTION  
The MIM Dual Degrees Capstone is an exciting hands on management course. Students will be assigned to teams, each of which will run a company and will compete with each other in a virtual but real market over several years.  
It is not only a course for students to practice the theory learned throughout the MIM program, but an immersive experience in which they will experience real strategic and tactic management and will develop critical managerial skills, such as analysis, decision making, controlling, communication at a senior level, teamwork and leadership.

## TEACHING METHODOLOGY  
IE University teaching method is defined by its collaborative, active, and applied nature. Students actively participate in the whole process to build their knowledge and sharpen their skills. Professor’s main role is to lead and guide students to achieve the learning objectives of the course. This is  done by engaging in a diverse range of teaching techniques and different types of learning activities.

The specific methodology employed is as follows:  
1. The MIM Capstone is a hands on experiential learning course based on the use of a highly advanced and realistic business simulation. Students will work in teams and will manage their own virtual company, applying business administration and management theory in a market in which they will compete with their classmates.  
2. As managers, they will experience the management cycle: analysis, planning, execution and control, and as part of it they will present before their company's Chief Executive Officer annual results and future plans. During these meetings they will analyze the company's performance and situation and will present goals, objectives, strategies, tactics, action plans and financial forecasts for the future.  
The simulation will be divided into two parts, referred to as cycles or rounds:

The methodology employed allows to practice what students learn during the program and to develop managerial skills.
