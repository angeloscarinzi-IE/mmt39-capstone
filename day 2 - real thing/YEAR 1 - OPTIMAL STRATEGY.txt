  FACTORY TO PLATFORMS — the rule is now confirmed, so this leg is solved
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Annual capacity per vehicle = 500 x load capacity in kg. It predicted both
  Year-1 outcomes exactly: market A carried 6,340,088 units on 8,500,000 of
  capacity and did not stock out; market B carried 8,089,912 on 7,500,000 and
  did.

| Market | Units to ship | Fleet         | Capacity   | Headroom | Cost       |
| ------ | ------------- | ------------- | ---------- | -------- | ---------- |
| A      | 11,912,819    | 2 x 16,000 kg | 16,000,000 | 34%      | EUR 86,400 |
| B      | 14,997,181    | 2 x 20,000 kg | 20,000,000 | 33%      | EUR 96,000 |

  ⚖️ One vehicle cannot do market A: the 24,000 kg legal maximum gives
     12,000,000 units against 11,912,819 needed — 0.7% of headroom, which is
     not headroom. Two vehicles each is the minimum, and it is cheap.

  ⚠️ Note market B is the LARGER market for us, not A. In the test round B took
     56.1% of our units on a smaller population. We sized A bigger last time
     and B is what stocked out.

  PLATFORM TO RETAILERS — the EUR 2.4M mistake, corrected
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

| ------ | --------- | -------- | -------------- | ------------- | -------- |

  🔴 In the test round we planned ONE vehicle per market and were charged

  ⚖️ 4,000 kg rather than 1,500: at this year's volume market B moves 132 units

  📌 The template default is 30 vehicles at 1,500 kg. On this leg the template

  WHY EVERY SALARY STAYS EXACTLY WHERE IT WAS
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  📌 THE PRODUCTION-WAGE FINDING IS THE IMPORTANT ONE. Section 31 warns that low

     operator EUR 13,500 and specialist EUR 24,000 the plant produced EXACTLY
     81.25% of nameplate — the theoretical maximum. There was no productivity
     loss to buy off. With 2,130 production staff at 22 lines on three shifts,
     holding the line here is worth about EUR 729,000 and it keeps the standard
     cost down, because these wages sit INSIDE the standard cost.

  ⚖️ Reps: market B ran HALF of market A's sales force in the test round and
     outsold it by 28.8%. So we hold A at its tested 4+4 and lift B from 2+2 to

     84 reps at EUR 26,000 = EUR 2,184,000 fixed, plus 0.6% variable.

  📌 Commission is paid on price-to-retailer, on channels S and T ONLY —
     hypermarket volume is commission-free. Verified: 0.6% of S+T net revenue is
     EUR 310,685, which is the exact figure the simulator charged in Year 1.
     Our variable bill this year: EUR 976,282.
